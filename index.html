Ali’s Personal Google API Client

A small OAuth client for accessing my own Google accounts from the command line.

What it does

I use this client with rclone to work with my files in Google Drive, and with occasional local scripts for my own Google API tasks. Depending on the tool and permissions granted, those tasks may include listing, reading, copying, uploading, syncing, or deleting my own data.

Who it is for

Just me: Ali Al-Alak. This is a personal utility, not a public application or a service offered to others. There are no registrations or sign-ins on this website.

How access works

Google’s authorization screen shows the permissions requested by the tool being used. Access is granted through Google OAuth, and credentials are used by my own tools. This static website does not receive OAuth tokens or Google account data.

Access can be withdrawn through Google Account connections. The privacy policy explains data handling; the terms of service explain the intended use.

Contact

For questions about this client or these policies, open a repository issue. Issues are public; do not include credentials, tokens, or private account data.
